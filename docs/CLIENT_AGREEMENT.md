## 1. What this agreement covers

1.1 We will provide the services in **Schedule 1**: a Finance Ops Audit, then one or more of our four core services (Invoice Automation, AI Phone Booking, Quote Calculator, Reporting Dashboard), set up for you and run as a monthly package. Monitoring, fixes and alerts (Monthly Operations Management) are included in every package.

1.2 Our systems sit on top of the tools you already use (for example Xero, your jobs sheet or job software, your calendar, phone line and email). You keep your own accounts for those tools.

1.3 We do **not** provide accounting, tax, legal, financial or investment advice. Payment chasing means polite reminders to your customers in your name; we do not act as a debt collector or take legal action. Reports and suggestions are information to help you decide; the decisions are yours.

## 2. How the work is done

2.1 **You stay in control of money.** Invoices we create in Xero are **drafts** until you approve them. We never send an invoice, take a payment or move money on your behalf unless you have approved it.

2.2 **AI is a helper, not a decision-maker.** Some steps use AI (for example checking unusual jobs, drafting quotes or summarising your week). Anything that affects money or a legal position is shown to you for approval first. AI can make mistakes, so please check what you approve.

2.3 **Everything is logged.** We keep a record of each automated action so you and we can see what happened and why.

2.4 **If something fails,** the system is set up so your customers are not lost: calls fall back to your reception number, failed jobs are held for review, and we are alerted. We aim to respond to failures within [one working day] (Complete plan: [four working hours]).

## 3. Fees and payment

3.1 Fees are set out in **Schedule 1**: a one-off setup fee per service, a monthly package fee, and any audit fee. Monthly fees are only sold as packages (Starter, Growth or Complete) and are set by your annual revenue band; setup fees do not change with business size.

3.2 **Audit fee.** The Finance Ops Audit is either free (where we have offered it free) or £100, paid by payment link before the audit is booked. A £100 audit fee is credited in full against your setup fee if you sign within 30 days of receiving the audit report.

3.3 **When we invoice.** Setup fees: 50% on signing and 50% when your systems go live, unless Schedule 1 says otherwise. Monthly fees: in advance, on the [1st] of each month. Payment is due within **14 days** of the invoice.

3.4 **VAT.** Keystone is a sole trader and **not VAT registered**, so no VAT is charged. If Keystone registers for VAT we will tell you in writing at least 30 days before VAT is added to new invoices.

3.5 **Third-party costs.** Your own subscriptions (for example Xero) stay yours. For AI Phone Booking, AI phone call costs above **500 minutes a month** are passed on at cost, shown on your invoice.

3.6 **Late payment.** In line with our halal policy **we do not charge interest**. If an invoice is more than 30 days late we may pause the services (after giving you 7 days' written notice) until it is paid. Paused services can be restarted as soon as payment is received.

3.7 **Price changes.** We may change monthly fees once a year with at least 60 days' written notice. You may end the agreement before the change takes effect (see clause 9).

## 4. What we need from you

4.1 Access to the tools we connect (for example adding Keystone as a user in Xero, sharing a jobs sheet, calendar access) and accurate information about your prices, services and rules.

4.2 A named person who can approve drafts, quotes and changes, and who reads our alerts.

4.3 You are responsible for the content you ask us to send in your name (prices, terms, greetings), for checking drafts before you approve them, and for your own legal duties to your customers and staff.

## 5. AI phone agent rules

5.1 **Honesty.** Our AI phone assistant never pretends to be a person. If a caller asks, it says it is an AI assistant for your business. It offers to put the caller through to a person.

5.2 **Where it can be used.** We only switch the AI phone agent on for phone lines and callers in the United Kingdom unless we have both agreed otherwise in writing. **We will not deploy it in the European Union, or in any US state, where the law requires AI systems to tell callers proactively that they are AI**, unless the greeting has been changed to meet that law and we have both signed off the change.

5.3 **Greeting and notices.** Your greeting must tell callers they may be speaking to an automated assistant and that calls may be recorded or transcribed for quality and record-keeping. We will set this up with you; you must not remove it.

5.4 **Emergencies.** The assistant is set up to pass urgent calls straight to your nominated number and give basic safety advice (for example to call 999 or the gas emergency line). **It is not an emergency service** and cannot guarantee that an urgent call reaches a person. You remain responsible for your own emergency cover.

5.5 **Bookings and prices.** The assistant books into the calendar you give us using your opening hours and job lengths, and gives prices only as estimates from the rules you approve.

## 6. Data protection (UK GDPR)

6.1 For your customers' and staff's personal data, **you are the controller and Keystone is your processor**. We process it only to provide the services and only on your documented instructions (this agreement and Schedule 2 are those instructions).

6.2 We will:
- keep personal data confidential and make sure anyone who handles it is bound to confidentiality;
- use appropriate security (access controls, encrypted connections, least-access accounts, logs);
- use only the sub-processors listed in **Schedule 2**, give you 30 days' notice of any new one, and let you object;
- where data leaves the UK (for example some US-hosted tools), rely on the UK's approved safeguards (an adequacy decision, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework);
- tell you **without undue delay, and within 48 hours**, after we become aware of a personal data breach, and help you meet your own reporting duties;
- help you answer requests from people exercising their data rights, and with impact assessments where reasonably needed;
- at the end of the agreement, return or delete your personal data within 30 days (unless the law requires us to keep it), and confirm this in writing;
- give you the information you reasonably need to show we meet these terms, and allow a reasonable audit once a year on 30 days' notice.

6.3 You confirm you have a lawful basis to give us the data and that your privacy notice tells your customers about the tools you use (including AI phone answering and call transcription).

6.4 We keep call logs, quotes and job records no longer than needed: by default **12 months for call logs and 24 months for quotes**, unless you ask for a different period or the law requires longer (for example accounting records you keep in Xero).

## 7. Halal-only client policy

7.1 Keystone only works with businesses whose main activities are halal. We do not work with businesses whose main activity involves pork, alcohol, gambling, interest-based lending, adult entertainment or other activities we reasonably consider haram (**Schedule 3**).

7.2 You confirm your business meets this policy and you will tell us if that changes. If it stops meeting the policy, we may end this agreement on 30 days' written notice, and we will refund any fees paid in advance for services after the end date.

## 8. Liability

8.1 Nothing in this agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot legally be limited.

8.2 We are not liable for:
- indirect or consequential loss, or loss of profit, revenue, business or goodwill;
- losses caused by information you gave us, drafts or decisions you approved, or your own tools and accounts;
- outages or changes in third-party services we do not control (for example Xero, phone networks, AI providers), though we will work to restore the service and keep callers and jobs safe;
- errors in AI-generated content that you approved or sent.

8.3 Our **total liability** under or in connection with this agreement in any 12-month period is limited to **the fees you paid us in that 12-month period**.

8.4 You must tell us about any problem promptly so we can fix it and limit any loss.

## 9. Term, notice and ending the agreement

9.1 **Minimum term:** 3 months from the go-live date. After that the agreement continues month to month (monthly rolling).

9.2 **Notice:** either of us may end it by giving **one month's written notice** (email is fine), to end after the minimum term.

9.3 Either of us may end it immediately by written notice if the other seriously breaks it and does not put it right within 14 days of being asked, or becomes insolvent.

9.4 **On ending:** we switch off the automations, hand over or delete your data (clause 6.2), and give you a copy of your settings (prices, rules, greetings) in a usable format. Fees for services already provided remain payable. Draft invoices already in your Xero stay there.

## 10. Ownership

10.1 Your data, your customer lists and your brand belong to you.

10.2 Our workflows, templates, prompts and know-how belong to Keystone. While the agreement runs you may use them for your business. They are not resold or shared.

10.3 With your written permission (Schedule 1), we may describe our results for you anonymously or as a case study. We will never share your customers' details.

## 11. Confidentiality

Each of us keeps the other's confidential information private and uses it only for this agreement, during it and for 2 years after it ends (longer for personal data and trade secrets).

## 12. General

12.1 **Changes** to this agreement must be agreed in writing (email is fine) by both of us.

12.2 **Complaints:** email [Keystone email]. We will acknowledge within 2 working days and aim to resolve within 10.

12.3 **Subcontracting:** we may use subcontractors and the sub-processors in Schedule 2, and stay responsible for their work.

12.4 **Whole agreement:** this agreement and its schedules are the whole agreement between us about the services.

12.5 **Law:** this agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales decide any dispute.
