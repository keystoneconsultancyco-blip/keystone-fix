Our approach

Three engagements. One outcome: revenue collected in full, and on time.

Most trades businesses generating between £2M and £10M in annual revenue have outgrown their administrative processes without rebuilding them. The following outlines exactly what each engagement involves, the problem it addresses, and the outcome it delivers.

01

Finance Ops Audit

Two-week engagement
THE PROBLEM

Jobs are being missed and invoices delayed, yet without precise measurement, neither the cause nor the value of any corrective action can be established with confidence.

WHAT WE DO

A rigorous analysis of call logs, invoice ageing and quote turnaround times produces a complete map of the cash-to-collection pathway, from first contact to final payment, revealing precisely where value is being lost.

WHAT YOU GET A number for every leak

For example: 18% of inbound calls go unanswered after 5pm; the average invoice remains unpaid for 34 days; quotes take 3.2 days to reach the customer. The result is a prioritised, evidence-based plan of action, ranked by financial impact.

02

System Setup

Four-to-eight-week build
THE PROBLEM

Administrative work consumes hours that should be devoted to revenue-generating jobs. Invoices are raised only when someone remembers to do so. Calls received after 5pm or on a Saturday go unanswered, and the opportunity passes to a competitor.

WHAT WE DO

We design, build and connect four software systems around the business's existing job management and accounting platforms: automated invoicing triggered by job completion, automated payment collection and follow-up, an intelligent telephony system that answers, qualifies and books jobs into the calendar around the clock, and automated quoting generated directly from the price book.

WHAT CHANGES 100% of calls answered

Invoices are issued the same day, rather than five to seven days later. Quotes are delivered within the hour, rather than two to three days later. On average, 10 to 15 administrative hours are returned to the business each week.

03

Monthly Ops Management

Ongoing retainer
THE PROBLEM

Systems require maintenance to remain effective. Price lists change, a new team member is not added to the phone rota, a payment provider updates its interface, and without dedicated ownership, an automated process quietly reverts to a manual one within months.

WHAT WE DO

We monitor and maintain the collections cadence, telephony rules, invoicing logic and quoting templates, refining each as the business evolves. A monthly report benchmarks performance against the original audit findings.

WHAT YOU GET Under 14 days to pay

Average days to payment held under 14. Missed call rate held under 2%. A single monthly report provides complete visibility of cash position and administrative performance, with no surprises at month end.
