How we work

Three engagements. One outcome — money in, on time, without you chasing it.

Most trades businesses at £2M–£10M have outgrown their admin process but haven't rebuilt it. Here's exactly what each engagement does, the problem it solves, and what changes for you.

01

Finance Ops Audit

2-week engagement
THE PROBLEM

You know jobs are getting missed and invoices are going out late, but you don't have numbers — so you can't tell your office manager what to fix, and you can't tell if a fix even worked.

WHAT WE DO

We pull your call logs, invoice aging, and quote turnaround times and map the whole cash-to-collection path — from the phone ringing to the invoice being paid — and show you exactly where it breaks down.

WHAT YOU GET A number for every leak

E.g. "18% of inbound calls go unanswered after 5pm," "average invoice sits 34 days before payment," "quotes take 3.2 days to reach the customer." A prioritised fix list, ranked by cash impact.

02

System Setup

4–8 week build
THE PROBLEM

Admin eats the hours that should go to jobs. Invoices go out whenever someone remembers to raise them. Calls after 5pm or on a Saturday go to voicemail — and the customer rings the next plumber on Google.

WHAT WE DO

We build and connect four systems around your existing job management and accounting software: automated invoicing triggered by job completion, automated payment collection and chasing, an AI phone line that answers, qualifies and books jobs into your calendar 24/7, and automated quoting from your price book.

WHAT CHANGES 100% of calls answered

Invoices sent same-day instead of 5–7 days later. Quotes out within the hour instead of 2–3 days. Typically 10–15 admin hours a week given back to the office.

03

Monthly Ops Management

Ongoing retainer
THE PROBLEM

Systems drift. Price lists change, a new starter isn't added to the phone rota, a payment provider updates its API — and without someone owning it, "automated" quietly turns back into manual within a few months.

WHAT WE DO

We monitor and maintain the collections cadence, phone booking rules, invoicing logic and quoting templates, adjusting them as your business changes. You get a monthly report benchmarked against your original audit numbers.

WHAT YOU GET Under 14 days to pay

Average days-to-payment held under 14 days. Missed-call rate held under 2%. A single monthly report so you always know where cash and admin actually stand — no surprises at month-end.
