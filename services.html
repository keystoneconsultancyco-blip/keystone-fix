Our approach

Five services. One outcome: revenue collected in full, and on time.

Most trades businesses generating between £2M and £10M in annual revenue have outgrown their administrative processes without rebuilding them. Each service below addresses a specific point where revenue is being lost. Tap any one of them for an instant, personalised estimate.

01 Two-week engagement

Finance Ops Audit

A two-week diagnostic review of call handling, invoicing and collections that produces a precise, evidence-based map of where revenue is being lost.

The impact

Past audits have uncovered figures such as 18% of after-hours calls going unanswered and invoices sitting unpaid for 34 days on average, the kind of leak that stays invisible without precise measurement.

02 Four-to-eight-week build

System Setup

We design, build and connect the systems that automate invoicing, collections, phone booking and quoting around your existing job management platform.

The impact

Clients typically recover 10 to 15 administrative hours each week, with invoices issued the same day rather than five to seven days later and quotes reaching customers within the hour instead of two to three days.

03 Ongoing retainer

Invoice + Collections

An ongoing retainer that manages automated invoicing and payment collection, so invoices go out the moment a job is complete and follow-up happens without anyone chasing.

The impact

Retained clients hold average days to payment under 14, a marked improvement in cash flow achieved without adding headcount.

04 Ongoing retainer

Phone Booking

An ongoing retainer for the intelligent telephony system that answers, qualifies and books jobs into the calendar at any hour, including evenings and weekends.

The impact

Clients on this retainer hold missed call rates under 2%, capturing after-hours enquiries that would otherwise go to a competitor.

05 Ongoing retainer

Full Bundle

The complete retainer covering invoicing, collections and phone booking together, monitored and refined monthly against your original audit findings.

The impact

The combined effect compounds across all three systems, with a single monthly report showing exactly where cash position and administrative performance stand.
