Our approach

Three engagements. One outcome: revenue collected in full, and on time.

Most trades businesses generating between £2M and £10M in annual revenue have outgrown their administrative processes without rebuilding them. The following outlines exactly what each engagement involves, the problem it addresses, and the outcome it delivers.

01

Finance Ops Audit

Two-week engagement
THE PROBLEM

Jobs are being missed and invoices are going out late, yet without the numbers to prove it, it is not possible to identify what needs fixing or confirm whether a fix has worked.

WHAT WE DO

We analyse call logs, invoice ageing and quote turnaround times to map the complete cash to collection path, from the initial call to final payment, identifying precisely where it breaks down.

WHAT YOU GET A number for every leak

For example: 18% of inbound calls go unanswered after 5pm; the average invoice remains unpaid for 34 days; quotes take 3.2 days to reach the customer. A prioritised list of corrective actions, ranked by cash impact, follows.

02

System Setup

Four-to-eight-week build
THE PROBLEM

Administrative tasks consume hours that should be spent on jobs. Invoices are issued only when someone remembers to raise them. Calls received after 5pm or on a Saturday are directed to voicemail, and the customer proceeds to call the next available provider.

WHAT WE DO

We build and connect four systems around your existing job management and accounting software: automated invoicing triggered by job completion, automated payment collection and chasing, an AI phone line that answers, qualifies and books jobs into your calendar 24 hours a day, and automated quoting from your price book.

WHAT CHANGES 100% of calls answered

Invoices are sent the same day, rather than five to seven days later. Quotes are issued within the hour, rather than two to three days later. Typically, 10 to 15 administrative hours per week are returned to the office.

03

Monthly Ops Management

Ongoing retainer
THE PROBLEM

Systems drift over time. Price lists change, a new starter is not added to the phone rota, a payment provider updates its API, and without someone responsible for maintenance, an automated process quietly reverts to a manual one within a few months.

WHAT WE DO

We monitor and maintain the collections cadence, phone booking rules, invoicing logic and quoting templates, adjusting them as the business changes. A monthly report is provided, benchmarked against the original audit figures.

WHAT YOU GET Under 14 days to pay

Average days to payment held under 14 days. Missed call rate held under 2%. A single monthly report ensures cash position and administrative performance are always known, with no surprises at month end.
